Chapter 01

Where it all
began.

In 2021, I walked into iTester Inc. as a Digital Marketing Intern — writing copy, managing social channels, learning how brands speak to people.

But somewhere between campaign analytics and customer conversations, I discovered something deeper: I didn't just want to market products. I wanted to build them. I earned my Google UX Design Certificate, transitioned into QA, and started sketching wireframes alongside test cases.

That dual lens — designer and quality guardian — became my superpower. Today, as QA Lead & Product Designer, I don't hand off designs and hope they work. I design them, test them, break them, fix them, and sign off on every release myself.

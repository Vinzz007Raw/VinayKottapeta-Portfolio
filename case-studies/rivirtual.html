01 · Challenge

Problem statement & business goals

10+ major releases — each adding modules without a unified design language

RiVirtual served real estate professionals across the USA and Canada, but CRM, property posting, and sign-in had grown organically — inconsistent, hard to scale, and split across eight user roles.

Problems identified

Fragmented workflowsLeads, listings, subscriptions, and onboarding lived in disconnected modules
Legacy screen debtOutdated UI created friction for realtors, customers, and internal teams
Multi-market complexityUSA & Canada localization layered onto UI not built for scale
No unified design systemTen platform versions shipped without cohesive components or patterns
Design–QA disconnectNo single practice accountable for experience quality across releases

Business goals

  • 01Unify CRM, property posting, and authentication under one design language
  • 02Redesign legacy flows to improve engagement and task completion
  • 03Scale role-based experiences across eight distinct user types
  • 04Establish design + QA as one accountable practice from v0.1 through v10.0

My role: Lead Product Designer & QA Lead — owning end-to-end design, test strategy, and release sign-off from v0.1 through v10.0.

02 · Research & Insights

Understanding the PropTech ecosystem gap

Research before redesign — mapping eight user roles, auditing legacy flows, and analyzing how multi-market real estate platforms scale.

01

Mapped journeys across eight user roles — from customers and realtors to lenders and advisors

02

Audited legacy CRM, property posting, and sign-in flows for friction and inconsistency

03

Analyzed USA & Canada localization needs including AI-assisted property titles

04

Defined a unified design system architecture to support ten platform versions

Key insight

Real estate platforms fail at scale when every module looks and behaves differently — users need one ecosystem that adapts to their role without relearning the product.

03 · Approach

Designer + QA Lead — one accountable practice

What made this engagement unique: I owned both the design vision and quality assurance. Every wireframe shipped with a test plan; every hi-fi screen was validated before production.

I mapped journeys across 8 user roles first, then redesigned module by module — CRM, property posting, authentication, subscriptions — while leading a team of 10+ testers and driving USA/Canada localization including AI-assisted property titles.

10 · User Roles & Flows

User roles & personas

Eight distinct roles across the RiVirtual ecosystem — each with unique goals, pain points, and color-coded journeys.

Role Primary Goal Key Pain Points Color
Customer / Property Owner Buy, rent or manage property Overwhelming choices, lack of trust Green
Realtor / Broker Generate & convert leads Lead leakage, manual follow-ups Teal
Builder / Developer Sell inventory efficiently Inventory management, lead quality Orange
Community / Property Manager Manage residential operations Communication gaps, manual processes Purple
Provider / Vendor Get service requests Low visibility, payment delays Pink
Lender / Financial Institution Originate & manage loans Risk assessment, documentation Blue
Project Manager Execute projects smoothly Coordination issues Green
Advisor (RiVirtual Associate) Facilitate deals & earn commission Limited tools & tracking Brown

User flow map & journey architecture

All users common flow

Landing Page → Explore / Search → Sign Up / Login → Choose Role → Onboarding → Role-Based Dashboard

5-stage platform flow

Stage Objective Key Activities
1 Discover & Onboard Awareness & setup Profile creation, verification, preference setting
2 Manage Operations Day-to-day platform use Property posting, lead management, inventory
3 Engage & Operate Daily interaction Chat, inquiries, showings, notifications
4 Transact Deal execution Offers, negotiation, E-Sign, payments, documentation
5 Grow & Analyze Performance & scaling Analytics, reports, reputation, referrals

Special Case Study · RiVirtual Only 12 · Digital Business Card

Networking modernized for real estate professionals

A personalized virtual business card — share contact info, listings, and QR access through a mobile-first, clickable interface. Free for agents, brokers, builders, lenders, and property managers.

10K+digital cards issued
60K+businesses on platform
100K+active users

The problem

Why paper business cards fall short in real estate.

Easily lostPaper cards get misplaced after a single meeting
Quickly outdatedContact details change but cards cannot update
No analyticsZero visibility into engagement or follow-up
Multi-step sharingSharing listings requires separate channels

How it works

Five steps from dashboard to a shareable, trackable digital card.

1

Access

Open your card link from the RiVirtual dashboard

2

View

See your branded card with photo, QR, and contact info

3

Interact

Tap CTAs — Text Me, Call Me, My Site, My Listings

4

Share

Download as PDF or share the QR code as JPEG

5

Update

Edit Profile changes sync to your card automatically

Social links configured in Edit Profile appear on the card — no manual re-entry needed.

Responsive designOptimized templates for mobile and desktop
Brand consistencyRiVirtual-powered branding with custom details

Impact

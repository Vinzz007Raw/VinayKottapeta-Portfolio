01 · Challenge

Digital brands need a physical voice

RiVirtual and iTester had powerful digital products — but conferences, investor meetings, and field sales still demanded something tangible. Something a prospect could flip through, keep on a desk, and remember after the tab closed.

Each brand needed its own print narrative: RiVirtual as a PropTech intelligence platform, the Top 25 Builder Awards as a prestige campaign, Ai Vidhya as an integrated test platform story, and iTester TF as a corporate capabilities brochure for AI, security, and testing services.

The challenge was translating complex product ecosystems into print-ready collateral — with distinct visual identities, clear information hierarchy, and production-quality layouts that felt as polished as the digital experiences they represented.

02 · Approach

One designer, four brand stories

I approached each brochure as a self-contained brand chapter — defining cover impact first, then building interior spreads that guide the reader from promise to proof.

For RiVirtual, that meant architectural photography, green brand accents, and PropTech positioning. For the Awards brochure, a premium gold-and-maroon award aesthetic. For Ai Vidhya, a neural-network visual language rooted in AI testing. For iTester TF, a bold monogram-led corporate system with gradient energy.

Every spread was designed print-ready — cover, inside panels, and back covers — with mockups created to present the final collateral as it would appear in a real-world sales or event context.

03 · Solution

Print collateral that carries the brand

Each brochure became a portable brand experience — designed to open conversations, build credibility at events, and extend digital products into the physical world.

RiVirtual's PropTech story and Awards campaign share a house-icon identity but speak in different tones — corporate intelligence versus ceremonial recognition. Ai Vidhya communicates technical depth through neural visuals and global reach statistics. iTester TF anchors the parent company with a confident monogram system and service-line clarity.

Together, the collection demonstrates range across luxury awards design, corporate tech brochures, and AI platform storytelling — all unified by intentional typography, color discipline, and production-ready layouts.

These brochures gave RiVirtual and iTester a tangible presence at meetings, conferences, and partner outreach — extending product brands beyond the browser into something prospects could hold, share, and remember.

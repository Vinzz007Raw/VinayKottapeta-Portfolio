01 · Challenge

Reputation at the speed of reviews

In a world where a single review can make or break a business, companies needed more than a dashboard — they needed an omnichannel reputation platform that could listen, respond, and grow across every industry vertical.

GoodReviews faced the dual challenge of building a scalable SaaS core — onboarding, pricing, subscriptions, payment — while also serving 24+ distinct industries, each with unique messaging, imagery, and customer expectations. Dental clinics, restaurants, yoga studios, and real estate agencies all needed tailored landing experiences within one unified product.

The frontend had to scale without fragmenting: one design system, one interaction language, but enough flexibility to make each vertical feel purpose-built. Engagement needed to grow dramatically without sacrificing consistency or conversion quality.

02 · Approach

One system, infinite verticals

I designed the entire frontend experience from the ground up — establishing a design system first, then building modular page templates that could adapt to any industry without reinventing the wheel.

Core SaaS flows — homepage, login, pricing, subscription, and payment gateway — were designed as the product backbone. Industry landing pages followed a repeatable template structure: hero, value propositions, social proof, and conversion CTAs — customized per vertical with industry-specific imagery and copy zones.

Analytics dashboards, campaign management modules, and review collection flows were designed alongside the marketing pages so the product felt cohesive from first visit through daily use. Every screen was tested for clarity, conversion, and visual consistency across the full 24-page library.

03 · Solution

Omnichannel reputation, beautifully scaled

GoodReviews gives companies the power to manage their reputation across every channel and every industry — through a SaaS platform built on one unified design language.

The homepage establishes trust and guides visitors to industry-specific landing pages. Login, pricing, and subscription flows create a frictionless path to activation. Payment gateway and failure-state screens handle edge cases with clarity rather than confusion.

Twenty-four industry landing pages — from restaurants and healthcare to real estate, beauty, and local services — each feel tailored while sharing the same underlying components. Analytics dashboards and campaign modules extend the experience beyond acquisition into daily reputation management.

04 · Impact

800% engagement growth, one design language

GoodReviews transformed from a concept into a scalable SaaS platform — 24 industry landing pages, a unified design system, and an 800% increase in engagement — all through intentional frontend design.

Businesses across every vertical now have tailored landing experiences that convert. The core SaaS flows — from onboarding through payment — provide a seamless activation path. Analytics dashboards and campaign modules extend value beyond the first click into ongoing reputation management.
